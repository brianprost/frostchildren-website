# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Frost Children fans, new listeners arriving from release coverage, and prospective
concertgoers. They visit primarily on phones and want to understand the current
era immediately, hear or buy the latest release, find a show, follow the band, or
join the mailing list.

## Product Purpose

The site is Frost Children's official, compact home on the web. It should make
the current release feel like an event while keeping the same practical content
available in every visual direction: band identity, release artwork, bio photo,
store and platform links, social links, tour dates, and mailing-list signup.

## Brand Personality

Aquatic, dangerous, and camp. The work should feel playful and physically
specific, with the strange confidence of a fashion image, a power tool, and a
chlorinated swimming pool occupying the same world.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic indie-band templates, centered link-in-bio stacks, polite festival
branding, nostalgia without a point of view, and visual indecision. Do not sand
down the artwork's absurdity or threat.

## Design Principles

1. Treat each release as a complete visual world.
2. Keep every practical destination easy to find inside expressive art direction.
3. Let the supplied photography do real compositional work.
4. Let expressive interactions follow familiar browsing behavior.
5. Preserve a quick, legible mobile path through the full site content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for essential text and controls. Support keyboard use,
visible focus states, semantic landmarks, descriptive image text, and a complete
reduced-motion experience. Do not rely on color, animation, or distortion alone
to communicate meaning.
